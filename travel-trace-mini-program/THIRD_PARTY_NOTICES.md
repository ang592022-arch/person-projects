## Natural Earth

The bundled world country boundaries and foreign city catalogue are generated
from Natural Earth 1:110m Admin 0 Countries and Natural Earth 1:50m Populated
Places.

## China boundary data

China province, prefecture and county boundary coordinates are intentionally
not included. The private prototype used a dataset whose original provider
record could not be verified, so that dataset is not redistributed here.

Consumers must obtain an authorized data source and complete any attribution,
map review or display obligations applicable to their deployment.
